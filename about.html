About

Hi, I’m Kuldeep, a footballer, a content creator and a writer. I love discovering new things.

I don’t enjoy being wrong, but when I am, I change my mind and update what I know without hesitation. I do my best learning in rooms where I’m not the smartest person, because I love learning from others.

I’m drawn to ideas and projects that could change human lives, which is why I’m working toward building something in sleep, so more people can rest better.

My mind sometimes takes odd turns. The story goes that James Watt got his steam engine idea from watching steam lift a kettle lid. That made me wonder whether we could harness something as vast as tectonic plate movement for energy. I write down every idea like this, in a notes app and on sticky notes by my bed. You can read more on my Writing page.
