Writing

So, I write regularly on LinkedIn, and I also write my own book drafts. As of now I have two book drafts - one on how situations in our lives are so similar to football and how we can learn to live our lives better from football principles. Then another draft is on the evolution of AI, which I wrote getting inspired by ‘Sapiens’ by Yuval Noah Harari. On Linkedin, I got regular postings because my mentor insisted me to grow a good audience there.

Then there’s my own notes app, where I write my own thoughts and my learnings private to me.

Writing in general makes me light-headed. It feels like I have taken out everything from my mind and put it on a paper or on my phone, I feel a sense of clarity.

I have sticky notes next to my bed, so whenever I am not able to sleep, which is very common, I write things that come to mind, like an idea, or something I have to do, or something I may forget later so I write it down so I don’t forget.

I have posted 30 posts in the last 2 months on LinkedIn.
