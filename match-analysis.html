Project

Match Analysis

I started this page because I have always been a football fan, I have played football since I was in 1st grade which is since 2013. I have lived football in and out. I have given so much time to football, be it from practice, playing matches, watching matches, thinking about football, talking about football etc. But I never thought about sharing my views to the public. I was camera shy, I still am but I am confident in telling people about my view on matches and tactics because it’s something I love and can talk about for hours. Then I met a Zayan through my mentor, we shared the mentor. He was so inspiring, he used to do stuff that he likes and not which will give him the most returns, doing things he likes the most with potential I believe. SO started this football page, when I record videos or put in work for the page it doesn’t feel like I am forcing myself to do it. I do it naturally, I enjoy it.

I usually watch matches, take my notes at half time and at full time. Sometimes I make previews before matches, but I only post them on stories and not as a reel/post.

I look for more context also, like the things happening before a game, than solely giving my opinion on the match itself.

My best reel was one where I tried a hook for the first time and was wearing Indian traditionals, because I had a garba night and a Barcelona match at the same time, I feel like people connected with its authenticity.

My worst reels were my initial ones because I was kind of shy, so wasn’t speaking with confidence, etc. But after 3-4 videos it became much easier to convey my ideas.

I noticed that becoming a creator isn’t that hard, if you do stuff that you already like and be real about it and not fake anything about yourself or the content you’re making, also having fun in the process, in the long-run you will definitely reach near your goals if not achieve them.

So, I only used to give my thoughts just after a game, take a camera just after the game is over, speak for 5 mins and post. But now I have started posting analysis of matches in a post format, like 10 images (carousel).

I also got into contact with barca creators who have 100K plus followers on Instagram alone. They gave me insights, and having your own USP or your own way of conveying your opinions and stuff is very important was among the insights they gave me. Such as @badbarcaopinions and others.

I approached these guys and asked for feedback because they already have this huge following, some replied some didn't.

I also contacted potential collaborators, like to feature with me on the page and make football content.

I currently have over 15+ posts, I started 2 months back. I have 250 followers. Total views of 50,000.
