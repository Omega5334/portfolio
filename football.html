Project

Football

I have played multiple ISSO (International Schools Sports Organisation), where 40+ teams used to compete. I have played Under-14, Under-17, and Under-19 in these categories. I was the captain for the Under-17 and Under-19 teams. When I look back I agree with my coach that I wasn’t the right captain material in my Under-14 days. I was selfish and used to care about my goals instead of the team’s performance. We won 1 Under-17 championship and one Under-19 Championship. All the other times we got eliminated in knockouts. I played in Under-14 ISSO at MIT Pune in 2019-20 season. We won the Under-17 championship in the 2021-22 season. We won the Under-19 Championship in the 2024-25 season.

I played in SGFI National games in 2025 as the captain of our team. We played against the best states in India including West Bengal, Gujarat, and Karnataka. We beat Karnataka Under-19 (3-1). For me it was a game I would never forget in my life, I didn’t score nor did I assist or delivered a crazy performance, but this games got the most passion out of me, I don’t remember myself that motivated and shouting like that ever before.

I kept reminding my team every 5 minutes that this is the game where we can beat a heavyweight like Karnataka, we will remember this game for the rest our lives, etc.

I participated in AIU in 2026 January (got eliminated in knockouts).

We won the Mahindra University Championship (it took place in 2026)

I did win Mahindra Cup - we played BITS Pilani, Mahindra University, etc.

I played in the Flame University Championship in 2026, got eliminated in the knockouts.

As captain I focused on only the things that matter and would affect my players other decisions. Like I during any tournament I insisted that wherever we go we go together, in that none of us ate unhealthy, got ourselves in any dangerous activities or games outside football, got better team harmony. Learnt more about each other, and built connections outside just football. I used to stay with the coaches after practice sessions to discuss team dynamics and how we should approach everything. I discovered the areas where I was weak and worked on them, such as I only used to speak with my players on the pitch or just before a game and not much outside the pitch. I changed that and got more social slowly and gradually it improved. There were some things I didn’t like, such as talking to my teammate’s parents because all of us were teenagers and I was the captain so I was the point of contact sometimes, it was kind of stressful.

My hardest moment was when there were conflicts in the team, players fighting for goals, not directly but we could sense it, outside football disagreements spoiling the dressing room atmosphere. I fixed these issues by consulting to each player individually and understanding multiple POVs. But in the end I just told everyone to fuck themselves because, what there life is outside football it doesn’t matter to me nor the team, it might to their friends on the team. I said that their issues can’t be big enough to ruin our efforts and that it can’t be the thing stopping us from winning titles, that would just be sad.

Yes, we won the Championship. But it took 2 tries, it didn't happen in the first go, we took our notes and got better in the second season.
