Writing

Books and LinkedIn

I write regularly on LinkedIn, where my mentor encouraged me to build an audience. I am also drafting two books. One explores how situations in life resemble football and how football principles can help us live better. The other covers the evolution of AI and was inspired by Sapiens by Yuval Noah Harari.

Notes and why I write

I keep a private notes app for my thoughts and learnings. Writing gives me clarity, as if I have taken everything out of my mind and put it on the page. I also keep sticky notes by my bed. When I cannot sleep, which is very common, I write down ideas, tasks or anything I might forget later.

LinkedIn and Substack

I have published 30 posts on LinkedIn in the last two months. I also started a Substack a month ago and have published three posts. I have not been as consistent there, and it is still a work in progress.
