Reading

What I have read

I have read Grit by Angela Duckworth, Captain Class by Sam Walker, Sapiens by Yuval Noah Harari and The Rational Optimist by Matt Ridley, among others. Sapiens is my favourite. It made me imagine how we evolved as a species and what we have built, and it showed me that nothing is impossible.

What I plan to read

Next, I plan to read the classics, such as The Wealth of Nations by Adam Smith, and physics books by Richard Feynman.
