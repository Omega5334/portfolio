Recovery

I tore my ACL in October 2023, in a morning training session. It showed my how weak I was, how I was not enough on my own. Outside the entire rehab, it was more of me spending time with myself. I isolated myself form the entire world, showing people that I am fine on my own. But that wasn’t good for me, I needed people I just didn’t have the courage to tell people that I need them to help me or simply just talk to me. I stopped talking to literally everyone even my own family. It was a bad period for me mentally. But physically I was devoting 4-5 hours everyday for 8 months to get back on the pitch and express myself fully again. It was tough from a physical pain point, especially the first 2 weeks after the surgery. But it always improves over time. I learnt so much, that you alone aren’t enough to achieve great things, you need others like I needed doctors and physiotherapists, coaches, athletes, my friends and family also. But once everyone’s work was done in my journey I didn’t look back and think about them again, I just thanked them and moved on, like their part in my journey was over. I know that I can always do more, I can always push myself to the limits. This period also pushed me to read books, which I can say without a doubt has been the best things I have done in the past few years. I became curious and read a lot of things. It was a bad phase and I overcame it. I won the Under-19 ISSO championship after my ACL rehab by the way, I also beat Karnataka Under-19 after my ACL too. I felt so proud of myself and saw how far I have come.

Sleep

Over time I have realised that sleep is so important, We spend 1/3 of our day and our resource which is the most fixed in nature ‘time’ sleeping and we don’t think about it much. I am saying all this and I probably do things for sleep that an average person does. The thing is we take it for granted, it is like a coping mechanism, for tiredness, for feeling sick, for feeling bad about something, or just a break from everything happening in your life. But it is one of the best investments I believe, it sets the tone for the entire day, and I have felt it, it’s true. I also bought memory foam pillows, red-light blocking glasses to sleep better. They help but I still am not satisfied with the sleep I get, I mean the quality of it. I also know it’s largely influenced by me watching matches at night, since European football matches are telecasted in India at 12 or 1 in the night. Or I just do music jamming sessions with my friends. So my body doesn't know when to sleep. I can say that if I had to solve any problem, having a better sleep would definitely be up there.
